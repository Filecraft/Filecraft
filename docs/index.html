04 / THE BEST UPLOAD IS NO UPLOAD

On your Mac.
End of story.

No account. No analytics SDK. No document server. No AI API. Just Apple’s on-device frameworks, doing the work where your files already are.

Small by construction.
No Electron. No bundled browser. No third-party runtime dependencies. Release-size budgets are enforced in the build, not left as a marketing promise.

Private, with clear boundaries.
Source EXIF and GPS dictionaries are not copied. Visible private content remains visible. Synced folders still sync. Prepare is not sandboxed or a redaction tool. Full boundaries ↗

Install with your eyes open.

Expand the ZIP and drag Prepare.app to Applications. This release is ad-hoc signed, not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, inspect the source and checksum before choosing whether to approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally. Managed Macs may not permit it.

What can it prepare?

1–20 single-frame JPEG, PNG or HEIC images, up to 40 MB and 80 megapixels each. No PDF inputs, OCR or searchable text layer. Check small text carefully after compression.

Public code. Ethical-source license.

Contributions are welcome. Prepare uses the Hippocratic License 3.0 core, with restrictions on harmful uses. It is not an OSI-approved open-source license. Read the license.

Build it yourself. Without a package hunt.

Prepare has no third-party Swift dependencies. You need Apple’s Swift toolchain, which is a separate, much larger download if not already installed. Build instructions ↗
