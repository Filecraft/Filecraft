# Support

I maintain Filecraft independently. Please use public issues for reproducible bugs
and discussions for questions: https://github.com/Filecraft/Filecraft/issues

Before reporting, read the platform limitations and try a synthetic JPEG/PNG.
Share OS/browser/build version, steps, expected result, actual result and whether
it also occurs with a synthetic fixture. Redact paths and personal information.
Do not attach private documents, identity records or credentials. There is no
paid support contract or response-time commitment.

For security vulnerabilities, use private reporting rather than a public issue:
https://github.com/Filecraft/Filecraft/security/advisories/new

Original Filecraft work in the 0.9 development line onward uses Apache-2.0.
Third-party components and build/test tools retain their own licenses. Earlier
releases retain their supplied terms; see [licensing scope](docs/LICENSING.md).
