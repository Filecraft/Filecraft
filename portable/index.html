    Local, with honest limits

    Extract this entire folder and open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox desktop browser. No server, install, account or internet connection is required. No analytics, uploads, external fonts, storage or automatic updates. This page blocks network connections with a Content Security Policy. Your browser, extensions, downloads folder, swap and synced folders have their own privacy behavior. Use a trusted browser without intrusive extensions.

    JPEG/PNG only; HEIC stays Mac-native. No PDF input, OCR or searchable text. Output is lossy raster PDF. Grayscale converts colors to equal RGB channels (not a dedicated DeviceGray PDF stream). Files are re-encoded without source EXIF/GPS dictionaries, but visible private details stay visible. Preparation is serial and limited, but the browser may decode a full image in memory. Cancel takes effect between decode/encode steps. Closing this tab releases the workspace; it is not autosaved.
